## Spotify → playable mapping (live)

154 tracks sampled from the account's 14 library playlists (972 tracks) were run through the app's
own `SpotifyMapper` + anonymous `YouTube.search`, exactly as `SpotifyPlaybackResolver` does:

- Before: Spotify's soundtrack credit stayed in the search query — `Solai Malai Oram (From
  "Villupaattukaran")` returned **zero** YouTube results, so the track could not be played.
- Now `SpotifyMapper.searchTitle` drops `(From "Movie")`, `(From Movie)`, `- From "Movie"`,
  `(feat./ft./with …)` and `- Remastered …` from the query, and scoring ignores the same credits.
- Result: **151 / 153** real tracks map above the 0.6 floor. The two misses are genuinely different
  YouTube titles (`Poongaatru Puthithaanathu` → `Poongaatru`, 0.56; `Suthi Suthi` →
  `Suthi Suthi Vandhiga`, 0.58). Blank-titled tracks now skip the search instead of a 400.
